# Technical Specification: Modular Agentic SDKs & Framework Integration

## Executive Summary
This document specifies the architecture, integration points, and implementation strategy for bringing Dr. Sri Amit Ray's Six Primary Mottos into modern AI agent frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, and Model Context Protocol servers).

## The Six Primary Mottos
1. **Relieve suffering**: Expand AI to ease individual, social, community, and planetary suffering.
2. **Create more jobs and new opportunities**: Foster meaningful work and sustainable skills.
3. **Serve where lives are at risk**: Support disaster response, search and rescue, and humanitarian relief.
4. **Apply No-Harm Principles**: Enforce mathematical floor vetoes on destructive actions.
5. **Protect dignity and choice**: Preserve human freedom, explainability, and override options.
6. **Care for Earth and future generations**: Maintain resource limits, CO2 budgets, and eco-mode execution.
